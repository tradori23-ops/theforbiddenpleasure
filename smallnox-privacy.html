1. Introduction

SmallNox is the official assistant bot for the LuxComics & MedusaComics community on Discord, related to noxmorningstar.com. SmallNox helps members discover comics, RolePlay content, community updates, events, music, guides, and server resources. SmallNox operates only within the official LuxComics & MedusaComics Discord server; it is not currently offered for installation on other, unrelated servers. This Privacy Policy explains what information SmallNox processes, why, and what choices you have. It applies to your use of SmallNox on Discord and does not cover Discord itself — see Section 8.

2. Who Operates SmallNox

SmallNox is operated by Sergio Tradori, an individual, under the project name LuxComics & MedusaComics (there is no registered company or business entity behind it) — referred to as "we," "us," or "the operator" in this policy.

3. Information We Process

Depending on which SmallNox features you use, we may process:

  • Discord identifiers: your Discord user ID, the server (guild) ID, channel IDs, and message IDs needed to respond to your commands or interactions.
  • Command interaction data: the slash command you used (for example /help, /comics, /roleplay, /music, /site) and any parameters you provided.
  • Message content: saved and processed when a specific feature technically requires it to work — for example, to respond to a message the feature is built around. We do not read or store messages outside what a feature needs.
  • Profile or preference information: yes — SmallNox saves and manages user profile information as part of its features.

We only collect what is necessary for the feature you are using. We do not use cookies or any similar tracking technology.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

  • Provide and operate SmallNox's commands and features;
  • Deliver the content or response you requested;
  • Maintain and troubleshoot SmallNox's functionality;
  • Help prevent abuse of SmallNox or the community;
  • Improve reliability where applicable.

5. How Long We Keep Information

We currently keep the information described above for an indefinite period — there is no fixed automatic deletion schedule at this time. If you would like your data removed sooner, see Section 7 (Your Rights) for how to request it.

6. Third Parties and Service Providers

We do not share, sell, or provide your information to any third party, except as necessary for SmallNox to function through Discord itself (Discord is covered separately in Section 8). We do not currently use any separate analytics, advertising, or marketing service provider.

SmallNox runs on Discord and is connected to the LuxComics & MedusaComics website, noxmorningstar.com, which is hosted on GitHub Pages with Supabase as its backend database and server-side functions. No other separate hosting, database, or analytics provider is used.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or request deletion of the personal data we hold about you, subject to applicable law and technical feasibility. To make a request, contact us at the email addresses in Section 13. Requests are handled directly by the operator (Sergio Tradori) by email, on a case-by-case basis — there is no separate ticketing system. Because SmallNox operates through Discord, some information (like your own messages) may also need to be removed directly through Discord's own tools, in addition to any request made to us. We will respond within a reasonable time and may need to verify your Discord identity before acting on a request.

8. Discord Is a Separate Platform

Discord is a third-party platform and is not operated by us. Your use of Discord itself — including the data Discord collects, how Discord handles messages, and Discord's own security practices — is governed entirely by Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, not by this document. We encourage you to review Discord's policies directly.

9. Age-Restricted (18+) Areas

Some areas of the LuxComics & MedusaComics community, and some content SmallNox may help you find, are intended for adults only (18+). Access to these areas is controlled by Discord's own age-restriction and server permission settings, not by SmallNox. Age-gating on Discord confirms a user has indicated they meet the minimum age — it does not verify identity or age in a legal sense.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information SmallNox processes. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's Privacy

SmallNox and the LuxComics & MedusaComics community are intended for users aged 18 and over. SmallNox is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a user under 18 has provided us with information, contact us using the details in Section 13 so we can address it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of SmallNox after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data can be sent to sergio.tradori@noxmorningstar.com or sergio.tradori@outlook.it.
